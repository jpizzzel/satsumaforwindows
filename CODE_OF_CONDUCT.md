# Code of conduct

Be kind. Everyone here is volunteering their time, and people of every background and experience level are
welcome.

- Disagree about code, not about people.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming the worst.
- No harassment, insults or personal attacks, in issues, pull requests or anywhere else connected to the project.

If someone makes you uncomfortable, report it privately through
[GitHub's security advisories](https://github.com/jpizzzel/satsumaforwindows/security/advisories/new) or by
contacting @jpizzzel on GitHub. I'll look into it, and I may remove comments or block people who don't follow this.
