      01 / How it works

      Three keys you already know

      Windows already uses Shift, Ctrl and Alt while you drag. Satsuma listens for them, opens a wheel right under the pointer, and gets out of the way the moment you let go of the key.

      Shift

      Convert

      Every format that makes sense for what you're holding. HEIC to JPG, MOV to MP4, a clip to a GIF, a PDF to images.

      the one you'll use daily

      Ctrl

      Tools

      Quick edits that save a copy. Compress a video for chat, merge PDFs, shrink a photo, strip its location data, zip a folder.

      originals never change

      Alt

      Actions

      The right-click stuff without letting go: copy, move, copy the path, share, open a terminal there, or park it in Float.

      04 / FAQs

      Questions people ask

      Why does Windows warn me when I open it?

      SmartScreen warns about any app that isn't code-signed yet and that it hasn't seen many times. Click More info, then Run anyway. Unblocking the zip before you extract it skips some of these warnings.

      Does it watch everything I do?

      It watches the mouse for one thing: a drag with Shift, Ctrl or Alt held. It never blocks or records input, and it never sends anything anywhere. Some antivirus tools get nervous about apps that watch the mouse at all, which is why a few of them flag it.

      Do I need to be online?

      No. Every conversion happens on your PC, including video and audio, through a copy of ffmpeg that comes in the zip.

      Where do converted files go?

      Right next to the original, by default. You can pick one folder for everything in settings instead. Click the tray icon to open them.

      Is it free?

      Yes, free and open source under the MIT license. The code is on GitHub, and bug reports, ideas and pull requests are welcome there.

      Is this Tangerine for Mac?

      No. Satsuma is an independent Windows app inspired by the same drag-and-hold idea. It isn't affiliated with Tangerine or LEAP STUDIO.
